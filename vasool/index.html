COLLECTIONS WORKFLOW

Designed around the work of getting paid

Rather than being another place to only write down balances, Vasool is being shaped around the follow-up steps that help merchants manage receivables.

₹

Customer dues in one place

Organise customer names, phone numbers, invoice references, amounts and due dates. The product requirements include manual entry and CSV or Excel import.

↗

Polite WhatsApp follow-ups

Plan reminders using approved message templates, suitable sending windows and frequency limits. WhatsApp provider setup and template approval are required before sending.

Payment-link workflow

A CLEARER ROUTINE

From a dues list to a follow-up plan

  1. Add customer dues manually or from a supported spreadsheet import.
  2. Review the message and confirm the customer relationship and permission to contact.
  3. Follow up respectfully using approved templates and configured rules.
  4. Track the outcome — payment, promised date, dispute or opt-out.

This describes the intended product workflow from the current requirements document. The exact steps available in a pilot may differ.

QUESTIONS

Vasool FAQs

What businesses should know about the current product direction.

What is Vasool?

Vasool is a multilingual payment-collections platform being developed by Rakya Services. It is designed to help businesses organise customer dues, manage polite payment follow-ups, track promised payment dates and view collection progress.

Does Vasool support Telugu, Hindi and English?

The product is being designed for Telugu, Hindi and English interactions, including Telugu replies typed using English letters. Exact language coverage will be confirmed during pilot validation.

Can Vasool send WhatsApp payment reminders?

WhatsApp follow-ups using approved message templates are part of the planned workflow. Availability depends on pilot readiness, provider setup, template approval and customer consent.

Can customers pay through UPI?

The planned workflow supports payment links, including UPI-enabled links. Payment provider configuration and settlement arrangements will be confirmed before a merchant pilot.

Can I import customer dues from Excel or CSV?

Importing customer dues from CSV and Excel is included in the product requirements. Ask Rakya about the import options available in the current pilot.

Is Vasool a lending or accounting app?

Vasool is focused on payment follow-up and collections workflow. The current product scope does not position it as a full accounting or ERP system, a lender, or a credit-scoring service.
