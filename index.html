FAQ

Common questions

I need a website but do not know what type. What should I do?

Just contact us with your business idea. We will suggest a static or dynamic website based on your needs and budget.

Can you work on both small and large projects?

Yes. We handle everything from a one page website to custom applications, dashboards and data pipelines.

How long does a website take?

A static website can often be ready within one to two weeks. Dynamic websites take longer depending on features.

Can you add AI to my website or business?

Yes. We can add chatbots, automate repetitive work and build AI features using Python and modern AI services. Tell us the task and we will suggest a practical solution.

What are data integrations?

They connect your different systems so information moves between them automatically. For example, website orders going straight into your accounting tool, or sales data feeding a Power BI dashboard.

What does DevOps do for my business?

DevOps automates how your software is tested and released. You get faster updates, fewer errors and a reliable setup on Azure, AWS or your own servers.

Will you help after the website is live?

Yes. We provide support, updates and fixes after delivery.

Do you also support people who are already working in IT?

Yes. We can discuss technical troubleshooting and guidance across supported technologies such as .NET, Python, SQL, Azure, DevOps, Power BI and APIs. Share your requirement so we can confirm the type of support available.
